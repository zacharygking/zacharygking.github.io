Selected work

Writing Tools · Prompt optimization · Evaluation

A Writing Tools feature, end to end

Owner prototyping through evaluation

I owned a Writing Tools feature through prototyping, data synthesis, prompt optimization, and evaluation. I built its per-locale optimization loop on the team’s platform (DSPy/GEPA) and gave each quality judge its own reward, so the optimizer could tell a near miss from an outright failure. I also built the evaluation datasets, benchmarked the automated grader against human grades, and adjudicated every disagreement. The grader proved more defensible than the human baseline, and the feature reached its shipping evaluation targets.

Writing Tools · LLM adapters

Multilingual Professional tone

20+ locales, shipped over two waves

I took over the Professional tone’s internationalization and adapter training. I built the data-synthesis pipelines, fine-tuned the LoRA adapters on locale data produced with localization partners, and took them through sign-off to release. They shipped in 20+ locales over two waves. Separately, I moved the Friendly tone onto new base models.

Safari · Knowledge graph

Safari Tab Group naming

ML + UI shipped on iOS and macOS

I mapped a web-directory taxonomy onto knowledge-graph entities so per-page topics rolled up into higher-level topics for grouping. I wrote the naming UI and the topic lookup, and shipped suggested Tab Group names on iOS and macOS.

Keyboard · Differential privacy

Privacy-preserving vocabulary learning

5 platforms shipped

I used differentially private on-device statistics to bring words the keyboard didn’t know, including names people type, into its lexicon. I won privacy and legal approval for it at a leadership review, and it shipped on 5 platforms.

Handwriting · On-device models

Handwriting language models

4 features shipped on its models

I extended the character n-gram pipeline to 13 locales and shipped its models behind Scribble, Live Text, Smart Script, and Visual Intelligence. The n-grams compressed about 290× on device.
