Selected work

Writing Tools · LLM adapters

Multilingual tone adapters

20+ locales, shipped over two waves

I owned the internationalization of the Professional tone in Writing Tools. I built the data-synthesis pipelines, fine-tuned the LoRA adapters on locale data produced with localization partners, and took them through sign-off to release. They shipped in 20+ locales over two waves. Separately, I moved the Friendly tone onto new base models.

Keyboard · Differential privacy

Privacy-preserving vocabulary learning

5 platforms shipped

I used differentially private on-device statistics to bring words the keyboard didn’t know, including names people type, into its lexicon. I won privacy and legal approval for it at a leadership review, and it shipped on 5 platforms.

Handwriting · On-device models

Handwriting language models

~290× on-device compression

I extended the character n-gram pipeline to 13 locales and shipped its models behind Scribble, Live Text, Smart Script, and Visual Intelligence. The n-grams compressed about 290× on device.

On-device ML · Evaluation

Neural topic classifier

Proposal → owner about ten months apart

I authored the dataset spec and architecture proposal for replacing a shipped Lucene/Java topic classifier with a neural one, along with the team’s multi-label evaluation-metric standard. About ten months later, I was named engineering owner of that replacement, sized to the Neural Engine’s on-device memory budget.
